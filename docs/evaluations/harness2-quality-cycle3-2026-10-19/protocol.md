## Question and diagnosis

Can an upstream-generated procedure preserve actors and actual negative spaces
more reliably, without sacrificing existing successes? Previous failures were
delivered, valid videos and passed the native visual reviewer, but lost source
patterns on moving actors. An independent examination of already revealed
artifacts and rendering traces will distinguish actual contour defects from
scorer ambiguity. Numerical historical measurements belong in the diagnostic
report, not in the optimization context.

The already disclosed cycle-2 creation task `media-90526c6f009fe0d09630` becomes
the one DEV reproducer. Its prior failed native rendering is evidence for
factual corrective feedback. It is explicitly exposed development material,
never fresh evidence again. Feedback can describe observed defects and cite
actual rendering code/SHAs. It does not provide a replacement candidate, grader
code, hidden masks, independent verdicts or measurement thresholds.

## Predeclared search and evaluation

- Real pinned Harness² sequential search, `k=2`: nominally eight DEV producers,
  six editing roles, one nontrivial selector and one reflection. Native selection
  may choose either composed final. No candidate is written by the operator.
- Fixed `gpt-6.1-sol`, low effort, existing ChatGPT subscription transport. No
  API billing, weight training or model/provider substitution.
- One DEV task; four regression tasks: cycle-1's two revealed holdouts,
  cycle-2's revealed repair holdout and the historical repair-310 task. These
  are four distinct exposed cases, disjoint from DEV.
- Six fresh holdouts: three creations and three repairs, generated once under
  a new persisted random namespace. Their order of kinds is creation, repair,
  repair, creation, creation, repair. No task is selected by its measured
  outcome. Source-image identities must be disjoint from every prior reserved
  or exposed example in this scope and from current DEV/regression.
- Original source generators, property checks, thresholds, visual reviewer,
  isolation and tool permissions remain unchanged. Local authored positive and
  semantic-negative controls must pass before the cycle is sealed.
- Caps: 32 producer reservations, 12 improvement-role reservations and 768
  improvement-tool calls, including retry headroom. Nominal comparison is 28
  producers (eight DEV plus two arms on ten independent tasks). Per producer:
  600 seconds, 64 tools, 16 renders and four reviews; per role: 300 seconds and
  64 tools. Native inference remains serialized. Subscription dollar cost is
  unmetered; elapsed time and tools are cost proxies.

The existing pipeline freezes the selected procedure before exposing the new
holdouts. Both independent arms receive the same task, model, tool access and
per-attempt budget, with predeclared alternating arm order. Every result,
terminal failure and unknown interruption stays in the ledger; failures are not
replaced with successful retries.

Admission uses the existing strict criteria: complete planned comparisons,
valid sources and conditions, complete candidate deliveries, same delivered SHA
passing separate visual and mechanical checks, no material visual defect,
strictly more joint successes on fresh holdout and zero lost baseline successes
in regression. Only planned task counts change from 2/2 to 4/6; the full policy
hash and implementation are sealed before search. No criteria or thresholds
will be adjusted after results are seen.

One improvement cycle is authorized for this experiment. Its outcome is retained
even if rejected. A measured win supports only this small synthetic task
distribution; six fresh tasks cannot establish broad media quality. Live
YouTube/Telegram/provider behavior and production rollout are outside this run.
